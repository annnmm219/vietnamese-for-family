# Vietnamese for Family — Southern Vietnamese Validation Pack

## What I need you to review

This course is for an **adult non-Vietnamese partner/spouse** speaking with a Vietnamese partner's parents, grandparents and extended family. Please review the Vietnamese below as **everyday Southern family Vietnamese, mainly Saigon / Ho Chi Minh City-oriented**, not as formal textbook Vietnamese.

**Nhờ bạn xem giúp:** những từ/câu dưới đây có phải là cách người miền Nam thực tế sẽ nói hằng ngày trong gia đình không? Nếu câu nào đúng ngữ pháp nhưng nghe quá sách vở, quá trang trọng, sai xưng hô, hoặc đơn giản là bạn sẽ không nói như vậy, vui lòng ghi **mã câu + cách bạn sẽ nói tự nhiên hơn**.

Please especially flag:
- unnatural or textbook wording
- wrong kinship/self-reference
- politeness that is too much or too little
- words that are not really Southern/Saigon everyday usage
- lines that need more context to be natural

Course assumption to validate: with the partner's parents the learner uses **con**; with the partner's grandparents the Southern track also uses **con** rather than the Northern-course **cháu**.

# Lesson 1 — Meeting the Family

### Core words
- **L1-S-01** `mẹ` — Mom / mother
- **L1-S-02** `ba` — Dad / father
- **L1-S-03** `con` — learner referring to themself in the family relationship
- **L1-S-04** `ông` — grandfather
- **L1-S-05** `bà` — grandmother
- **L1-S-06** `dạ` — polite acknowledgement

### Lines
- **L1-S-07** `Dạ, con chào mẹ.` — Hello, Mom.
- **L1-S-08** `Dạ, con chào ba.` — Hello, Dad.
- **L1-S-09** `Dạ, con chào ông bà.` — Hello, Grandma and Grandpa.
- **L1-S-10** `Dạ, con rất vui được gặp ba mẹ.` — I'm very happy to meet you both.
- **L1-S-11** `Dạ, con tên là [tên của bạn].` — My name is [name].
- **L1-S-12** `Hai đứa mới tới hả?` — Mother: You two just arrived?
- **L1-S-13** `Dạ, tụi con mới tới.` — Yes, we just arrived.
- **L1-S-14** `Dạ, con chào ba mẹ.` — Greeting both parents.

# Lesson 2 — Politeness & Natural Responses

### Core words / markers
- **L2-S-01** `dạ` — polite yes / acknowledgement
- **L2-S-02** `nha` — gentle sentence softener
- **L2-S-03** `dạ thôi` — polite no-thanks opening
- **L2-S-04** `cảm ơn` — thank you
- **L2-S-05** `xin lỗi` — sorry / excuse me
- **L2-S-06** `hiểu` — understand

### Lines
- **L2-S-07** `Dạ, con cảm ơn mẹ.` — Yes, thank you, Mom.
- **L2-S-08** `Dạ thôi, con cảm ơn mẹ.` — No thank you, Mom.
- **L2-S-09** `Dạ, con hiểu rồi.` — Yes, I understand now.
- **L2-S-10** `Dạ, để con làm.` — Okay, I'll do it.
- **L2-S-11** `Dạ, con xin lỗi, con chưa nghe rõ.` — Sorry, I didn't hear clearly.
- **L2-S-12** `Dạ, + [câu trả lời].` — course pattern for warming a short answer
- **L2-S-13** `Mai nhớ dậy sớm nha.` — Mother: Remember to wake up early tomorrow, okay?
- **L2-S-14** `Dạ, con nhớ rồi.` — Yes, I remember / got it.

### Core words

- **L3-S-04** `chén` — bowl
- **L3-S-05** `muỗng` — spoon
- **L3-S-06** `ngon` — delicious

### Lines
- **L3-S-07** `Con mời ba mẹ ăn cơm.` — Please eat, Mom and Dad / meal invitation.
- **L3-S-08** `Dạ, ngon lắm.` — It's very delicious.
- **L3-S-09** `Dạ, con no rồi.` — I'm full now.
- **L3-S-10** `Dạ, con ăn thêm một chút.` — I'll have a little more.
- **L3-S-11** `Dạ thôi, con cảm ơn mẹ.` — No more, thank you, Mom.
- **L3-S-12** `Dạ, con ăn rồi.` — I've eaten already.
- **L3-S-13** `Dạ, con chưa ăn.` — I haven't eaten yet.
- **L3-S-14** `Ăn cơm chưa?` — Have you eaten yet?
- **L3-S-15** `Ăn thêm đi con!` — Have some more!
- **L3-S-16** `Dạ thôi, con no rồi. Con cảm ơn mẹ.` — No more, I'm full. Thank you, Mom.

# Lesson 7 — Everyday Family Small Talk

### Core words
- **L7-S-01** `khỏe` — well / healthy
- **L7-S-02** `mệt` — tired
- **L7-S-03** `bận` — busy
- **L7-S-04** `hôm nay` — today
- **L7-S-05** `nóng` — hot
- **L7-S-06** `hơi` — a little / slightly

### Lines
- **L7-S-07** `Dạ, dạo này ba mẹ có khỏe không?` — How have Mom and Dad been lately?
- **L7-S-08** `Dạ, con vẫn khỏe.` — I'm still doing well.
- **L7-S-09** `Dạ, hôm nay con hơi mệt.` — I'm a little tired today.
- **L7-S-10** `Hôm nay nóng quá.` — It's so hot today.
- **L7-S-11** `Dạ, vậy hả?` — Oh, really? / Is that so?
- **L7-S-12** `Dạ, dạo này [người] có [tính từ] không?` — Has [person] been [adjective] lately?
- **L7-S-13** `Dạo này con có mệt không?` — Have you been tired lately?
- **L7-S-14** `Dạ, hôm nay nóng thiệt.` — Yes, it really is hot today.
- **L7-S-15** `Dạo này cô bận lắm.` — Aunt: I've been very busy lately.
- **L7-S-16** `Dạ, vậy hả? Cô có mệt không?` — Really? Are you tired?
- **L7-S-17** `Dạo này công việc có bận không con?` — Mother: Has work been busy lately?
- **L7-S-18** `Dạ, cũng hơi bận, nhưng con vẫn ổn.` — It's a little busy, but I'm okay.

# Lesson 9 — Tết & Family Visits

### Key terms
- **L9-S-01** `chúc Tết` — give New Year wishes
- **L9-S-02** `sức khỏe` — health
- **L9-S-03** `lì xì` — lucky money / red envelope
- **L9-S-04** `bàn thờ` — family altar
- **L9-S-05** `thắp hương` — light incense
- **L9-S-06** `xin phép` — politely excuse oneself / ask permission

### Visit flow
- **L9-S-07** `Dạ, con chào ba mẹ. Dạ, con chào ông bà.` — arriving and greeting parents + grandparents
- **L9-S-08** `Dạ, con chúc ông bà năm mới mạnh khỏe, vui vẻ.` — New Year wish to grandparents
- **L9-S-09** `Dạ, con làm thế nào?` — How should I do this? (when unsure about household ritual)
- **L9-S-10** `Dạ, con cảm ơn ông bà.` — Thank you, Grandma and Grandpa, for lì xì
- **L9-S-11** `Dạ, con xin phép ba mẹ, tụi con về.` — Mom and Dad, please excuse us; we're heading home.
- **L9-S-12** `Dạ, con chào ông bà.` — greeting grandparents
- **L9-S-13** `Dạ, con cảm ơn bà.` — thanking Grandma
- **L9-S-14** `Hai đứa mới tới hả? Vô chúc Tết ông bà đi con.` — Mother: You two just arrived? Go in and wish the grandparents a happy New Year.
- **L9-S-15** `Dạ. Con chúc ông bà năm mới mạnh khỏe, vui vẻ.` — response / New Year wish

# Lesson 10 — Surviving a Family Gathering

### Scene 1 — Arrival
- **L10-S-01** `Hai đứa mới tới hả? Vô đây con.` — Mother: You two just arrived? Come in.
- **L10-S-02** `Dạ, tụi con mới tới. Con chào ba mẹ.` — We just arrived. Hello, Mom and Dad.

### Scene 2 — Food
- **L10-S-03** `Ăn thêm đi con!` — Mother: Have some more!
- **L10-S-04** `Dạ thôi, con no rồi. Con cảm ơn mẹ.` — No more, I'm full. Thank you, Mom.

### Scene 3 — Personal question
- **L10-S-05** `Khi nào hai đứa cưới?` — Aunt: When are you two getting married?
- **L10-S-06** `Dạ, chuyện đó tụi con vẫn đang tính.` — We're still thinking about that.

### Scene 4 — Teasing
- **L10-S-07** `Nói tiếng Việt khá quá, sắp thành người Việt rồi đó!` — Uncle, teasing: Your Vietnamese is pretty good. You're almost Vietnamese already!
- **L10-S-08** `Dạ chưa đâu, con còn phải học nhiều.` — Not yet, I still have a lot to learn.

### Scene 5 — Overlapping conversation
- **L10-S-09** `Ăn thêm không?` — Mother: Want some more?
- **L10-S-10** `Dạo này công việc có bận không?` — Aunt: Has work been busy lately?
- **L10-S-11** `Mai hai đứa định đi đâu?` — Father: Where are you two going tomorrow?
- **L10-S-12** `Dạ, mai tụi con định đi chơi một chút.` — Tomorrow we're planning to go out for a bit.

### Scene 6 — Toast
- **L10-S-13** `Nào, chúc sức khỏe cả nhà!` — Uncle: Come on, good health to the whole family!
- **L10-S-14** `Dạ, chúc sức khỏe cả nhà!` — Good health to the whole family!

### Scene 7 — Leaving
- **L10-S-15** `Về luôn hả? Ở lại thêm chút đi con.` — Mother: Leaving already? Stay a little longer.
- **L10-S-16** `Dạ, hôm nay tụi con xin phép về. Con cảm ơn ba mẹ.` — We'll excuse ourselves and head home today. Thank you, Mom and Dad.
